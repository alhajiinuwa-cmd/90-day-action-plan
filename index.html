Chairman’s Directive

By 31 January 2027, every shop must be clean, properly stocked, financially accountable, compliant with approved company policies and demonstrating measurable improvement in sales and profitability.

  • No unrecorded sales.
  • No unauthorized credit.
  • No expired or non-compliant products offered for sale.
  • No unexplained cash or inventory losses.
  • Every transaction must be traceable.
  • Every branch must produce reliable management information.

Sales accountability rests with Branch Managers. Compliance verification is independent.

Programme Overview

Month 1 — November: Sanitize and secure. Eliminate expired/non-compliant products, reconcile inventory, establish financial controls, clean premises.

Month 2 — December: Standardize and grow. Make controls routine, improve merchandising, launch structured sales campaigns, strengthen retention.

Month 3 — January: Audit, optimize and sustain. Independently verify compliance, measure results, eliminate recurring weaknesses, lock in permanent standards.

Important Policy Correction

Discrepancy identified:
Internal Control Manual requires 18 months remaining shelf life.
Revised Job Description requires 12 months.

Recommendation: Formally amend Section 5.7 of the Manual to reflect the approved 12-month policy, with documented exceptions for products whose normal manufacturer shelf life is shorter. Communicate the updated policy to all receiving staff before implementation.

The minimum shelf-life rule applies at procurement or receipt. Existing stock should be separately assessed for expiry, condition and suitability for continued sale.
